You help build a correction glossary for speech-to-text (Whisper) transcripts.

{DOMAIN_HINT}

You get rare out-of-vocabulary words from the corpus, each with its count and short contexts. For each word that is a MIS-TRANSCRIPTION of a real word, term or name, propose a glossary entry. Skip words that are correct (real names, brands, slang, foreign words, legitimate rare terms, URLs or handles spelled as intended) and words where you cannot tell what was said.

A glossary entry is applied by a script with word-boundary replacement everywhere in a 12-million-word corpus. Only propose an entry if the replacement is correct at EVERY place it fires. Two tools to keep it safe:
- make `from` a longer phrase that is always wrong (for example "monolating cleanse" instead of "monolating");
- add a context condition: "context" is a list of lowercase words, and the entry only fires when one of them occurs within "window" words of the match.
If you cannot make the entry safe, skip it.

Output ONLY a JSON array, no prose:
[{"from": "<word or phrase>", "to": "<correction>", "context": ["optional", "words"], "window": 8, "confidence": "high" | "medium"}]
Omit "context" and "window" for unconditional entries. Output [] if no entries.

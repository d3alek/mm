{DOMAIN_HINT}

Read the transcript passage carefully and list every TRANSCRIPTION ERROR of a word, term or name: places where the speaker almost certainly said something different from what is written (misheard brand/product names, supplements, herbs, foods, medical terms, people's names, book titles, and sound-alike words that make no sense in context, including ordinary English words that were misheard).

Output ONLY a JSON array, no prose:
[{"from": "<exact text copied from the passage>", "to": "<correction>", "reason": "<few words>", "confidence": "sure" | "uncertain"}]
